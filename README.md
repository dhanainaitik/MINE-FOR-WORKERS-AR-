# ⛏️ MineSafe AR
### 🚀 AR-Based Coal Mine Safety Training Simulator

🎬 **Watch Project Demo:**  
https://youtu.be/P7jjGbIHY38?si=FiLMS9ICc3LoXszB

🛡️ **Enter. Experience. Respond. Learn.**

MineSafe AR is an Android-based Augmented Reality (AR) simulator that enables mine workers to practice emergency-response procedures inside an immersive virtual underground mine.

## 🚨 The Problem
Traditional mine safety training is often theoretical, limiting workers' opportunities to practice realistic emergencies in a safe environment.

## 💡 Our Solution
MineSafe AR transforms a physical room into a virtual underground mine, allowing workers to experience emergency scenarios, practice safety procedures, and receive performance feedback.

## 🎮 Key Features

🔥 **Electrical Fire Safety** — Practice fire detection, extinguisher handling, and emergency response.

☣️ **Chemical Hazard Safety** — Learn hazard identification, safe distancing, and reporting procedures.

🥽 **Immersive AR Environment** — Enter a virtual mine through an AR doorway and explore connected underground tunnels.

🚶 **Movement Amplification** — Approximately 1 metre of physical movement represents 10 metres of virtual movement.

🗣️ **AI-Assisted Voice Guidance** — Multilingual instructions and interactive safety explanations.

📊 **Performance Tracking** — Monitor response time, mistakes, completed steps, and safety scores.

## ⚙️ Technology Stack
📱 Android | Kotlin | ARCore  
🧊 GLB/glTF 3D Assets | Meshy | Blender  
💻 Antigravity IDE | Gradle

## 🔮 Future Scope
☁️ Backend integration  
👷 Worker profiles and training history  
📈 Admin dashboard and performance analytics  
🌐 Personalized, multilingual safety training

## 🏆 Project
Developed as a **Smart India Hackathon (SIH) project**, MineSafe AR aims to make mine safety training more immersive, repeatable, and measurable.

⚠️ *MineSafe AR complements official safety training; it does not replace certified procedures, supervision, or site-specific emergency protocols.*
